THE 10-AGENT TIPPING POINT

Why Autonomous Swarms Break Down

One AI agent in your editor feels like magic. Ten concurrent agents across separate worktrees create an unobservable, expensive black box.

✕ Without RigCommander: Chaos
  • • Blind Terminal Hopping: Scrambling through 12 detached tmux panes trying to figure out which agent is blocked or waiting in queue.
  • • Silent Permission Hangs: An agent sits idle for 4 hours waiting for a numbered confirmation prompt you never saw.
  • • Circular Rework Loops: QA rejects coder's PR 5 times in a loop, burning 300,000 tokens without anyone noticing wait bottlenecks.
  • • Blind Token Spend & Unverified Commands: No visibility into per-seat `$` burn, cache-hit rates, or whether `/compact` actually reduced context.
✓ With RigCommander: Deterministic Control
  • • Single-Pane Graph & Task Gantt: Switch between live SVG call highways and chain-colored Gantt timelines (`G`) separating wait from active work.
  • • Clickable HITL & Postcondition Audit: Auto-parsed tmux options, per-seat audio alerts, and automatic postcondition verification (`✅ verified`).
  • • Operational Ontology v2.0: Reason-driven Action Registry (0 noisy buttons on idle seats) backed by 7 canonical object types and unified P0–P3 triage.
  • • Live FinOps & Exclusive Pools: Incremental JSONL token reader with cache-hit %, per-seat `$` estimates, and non-overlapping model reservoirs.
